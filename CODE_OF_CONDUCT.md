# Code of Conduct

Orlo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/carbongotfound). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content or block anyone who doesn't follow these rules.
